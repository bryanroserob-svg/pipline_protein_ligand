## Context of use

This pipeline was developed and applied in the undergraduate thesis: *"Caracterización termodinámica in silico de tetralonas como inhibidores de BCL-2, Caspasa-9 y p53 mediante dinámica molecular, MM-PBSA y cálculos alquímicos de energía libre (RBFE/ABFE) en GROMACS"* (Escuela Superior Politécnica del Litoral, ESPOL, 2026).

It was used to conduct protein-ligand MD simulations of tetralonic compounds bound to BCL-2, Caspase-9, and p53, followed by MM-PBSA binding free energy calculations to thermodynamically characterize each complex and rank candidate inhibitors.
